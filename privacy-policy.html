Privacy Policy

AllCryptoTokens

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Introduction

AllCryptoTokens respects user privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when using AllCryptoTokens, including information processed by third-party services used to provide certain app features.

AllCryptoTokens does not require account registration or the submission of personal information to access its core features.

2. Information Collection

AllCryptoTokens does not directly request personal information such as a name, home address, telephone number, or other personal identification information.

Third-party services used by AllCryptoTokens may automatically process certain technical information when their services are accessed. Depending on the service, this may include:

  • IP address and network information;
  • device and operating system information;
  • advertising or device identifiers;
  • app interactions and usage information;
  • diagnostic and performance information;
  • information related to Google Play purchases.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to the applicable provider's privacy policy.

3. Local App Data

Certain app preferences, including favorite tokens, are stored locally on the user's device.

AllCryptoTokens does not require an account to store these preferences.

4. Internet Access and Market Data

Some features require an internet connection.

Cryptocurrency market information is delivered through AllCryptoTokens backend infrastructure using market data supplied by Coinranking and infrastructure provided by Cloudflare.

Network communications may involve technical information such as IP addresses and request metadata as necessary to deliver requested information and operate the underlying services.

Market information may be delayed and should not be considered real-time financial data.

5. Advertising

AllCryptoTokens uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP addresses, device information, advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and service operation.

Advertising data is processed according to Google's applicable privacy policies and may also depend on factors such as region, device settings, and consent choices.

Advertisements are not displayed in AllCryptoTokens while an applicable ad-free purchase or subscription is active.

6. In-App Purchases

AllCryptoTokens offers optional purchases through Google Play Billing, including monthly, annual, and one-time options for an ad-free experience.

Payments are processed by Google Play.

AllCryptoTokens does not directly receive or store complete payment card information.

Purchase-status information may be used by the app to determine whether an applicable purchase or subscription is active.

Payment processing, subscription management, cancellations, and applicable refunds are handled through Google Play according to Google's policies.

7. YouTube and External Links

AllCryptoTokens may provide access to educational videos hosted on YouTube.

Accessing YouTube content may result in information being processed by Google according to Google's applicable privacy policies.

AllCryptoTokens also provides links to cryptocurrency project websites and other external resources.

Opening an external website or service is subject to that service's own privacy policy and terms.

AllCryptoTokens is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of independent third-party websites and services.

8. Use of Information

Information processed through AllCryptoTokens or its third-party services may be used to:

  • provide market information and online functionality;
  • maintain app functionality;
  • preserve locally stored preferences;
  • display and measure advertisements;
  • verify optional purchases and subscriptions;
  • maintain security and prevent abuse;
  • diagnose technical problems and improve reliability;
  • respond to user communications.

AllCryptoTokens does not sell personal information obtained through direct user communications.

9. Data Storage and Retention

Preferences such as favorite tokens are stored locally on the user's device.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to their respective privacy and retention policies.

Google Play maintains applicable purchase and subscription records according to its own policies.

When AllCryptoTokens is contacted by email, the email address, message, and related correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to the inquiry or provide support.

10. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to support the security and integrity of AllCryptoTokens and its associated online services.

No method of electronic transmission, processing, or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-party providers are responsible for security practices applicable to information processed through their respective services.

11. Third-Party Services

AllCryptoTokens uses or provides access to third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies.

These services include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play and Google Play Billing
  • YouTube
  • Coinranking
  • Cloudflare

Users may review the privacy policies of these providers for additional information about their data practices and available privacy controls.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to AllCryptoTokens, third-party services, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

The Last updated date will be revised when changes are published.
